# Protocol: Greek Yogurt Vanilla Bean Ice Cream (Ninja Creami)

**Category:** desserts
**Tags:** #high-protein #batch-prep #low-mess
**Status:** BETA
**Version:** v0.1
**Servings / Yield:** 1 pint (4 servings, ~75g each)
**Prep time:** 5 min
**Freeze time:** 24 hours
**Tools:** Ninja Creami, blender

## Goal / Why this exists

Testing Greek yogurt as a protein-rich ice cream base without protein powder. Vanilla bean is a clean flavor to isolate the base characteristics — tang level, creaminess, iciness.

## Hypothesis

Greek yogurt + coconut fat can deliver creamy texture and decent protein without protein powder. The tang may work as a feature rather than a bug.

## Nutrition (per serving, ~75g)

- **Calories:** ~70
- **Protein:** ~6g
- **Carbs:** ~9g
- **Fat:** ~1.5g

*Note: Using 0% Greek yogurt. Fat content would increase with whole milk yogurt.*

## Ingredients

- 180g Greek yogurt (0% fat)
- 60g lite coconut milk (or 45g full-fat coconut cream)
- 35g allulose
- 5g vanilla extract (1 tsp)
- 1g vanilla bean seeds (optional, ~1/2 pod)
- Pinch kosher salt (Diamond Crystal)

## Method

1. **Combine** yogurt and coconut milk in blender. Blend until smooth.
2. **Add** allulose, vanilla extract, vanilla bean seeds (if using), and salt.
3. **Blend** until fully incorporated, ~30 seconds.
4. **Pour** into Ninja Creami pint container to max fill line.
5. **Freeze** for 24 hours minimum. Must be frozen solid.
6. **Process** on ICE CREAM setting.
7. **Check texture.** If icy, run RE-SPIN. Low fat base may need 2 spins.
8. **Serve immediately** for soft-serve, or freeze 1–2 hours for scoopable.

## Success criteria

- Creamy texture, not icy or crystalline
- Noticeable but pleasant tang (frozen yogurt character)
- Clean vanilla flavor comes through
- No off-flavors from coconut

## Variables to track

- Iciness level (1-10, 1 = smooth, 10 = crunchy ice)
- Tang level (1-10, 1 = undetectable, 10 = sour)
- Number of spins needed
- Texture after 2 hours in freezer (scoopable or brick?)

## Potential adjustments

- If too icy: increase coconut milk to 75g, or switch to full-fat coconut cream
- If too tangy: increase allulose to 40g, or add 15g honey
- If too thin: add 0.5g xanthan gum before blending

## Storage & reheat

- Keeps in freezer up to 2 weeks
- Will likely freeze solid — RE-SPIN before serving

# Protocol: Greek Yogurt Rich Chocolate Ice Cream (Ninja Creami)

**Category:** desserts
**Tags:** #high-protein #batch-prep #low-mess
**Status:** BETA
**Version:** v0.1
**Servings / Yield:** 1 pint (4 servings, ~95g each)
**Prep time:** 10 min
**Freeze time:** 24 hours
**Tools:** Ninja Creami, blender, small saucepan or microwave

## Goal / Why this exists

Rich, fudgy chocolate ice cream using Greek yogurt as a protein base. Solves the "cocoa powder isn't rich enough" problem by adding real chocolate (baking chocolate or sugar-free chips) for cocoa butter content and depth.

## Hypothesis

Melting real chocolate into the fat component (coconut cream) reintroduces cocoa butter that's missing from cocoa powder alone. The yogurt tang should read as "dark chocolate" or "chocolate cheesecake" rather than off-putting.

## Nutrition (per serving, ~95g)

- **Calories:** ~110
- **Protein:** ~7g
- **Carbs:** ~10g
- **Fat:** ~6g

*Note: Using 0% Greek yogurt. Fat comes from coconut cream and baking chocolate.*

## Ingredients

- 180g Greek yogurt (0% fat)
- 45g full-fat coconut cream
- 20g unsweetened baking chocolate (or 30g sugar-free chocolate chips)
- 15g Dutch-process cocoa powder
- 35g allulose
- 2g instant espresso powder
- 3g vanilla extract (1/2 tsp)
- Pinch kosher salt (Diamond Crystal)

## Method

1. **Warm** coconut cream in microwave or small saucepan until just simmering (~30 seconds microwave).
2. **Add** chopped baking chocolate to warm coconut cream. Stir until fully melted and smooth.
3. **Add** cocoa powder and espresso powder to chocolate mixture. Whisk until no lumps remain.
4. **Let cool** to room temperature (5-10 min). Don't skip — hot mixture will curdle yogurt.
5. **Combine** chocolate mixture with yogurt, allulose, vanilla, and salt in blender.
6. **Blend** until completely smooth, ~30 seconds.
7. **Taste** for sweetness — adjust allulose if needed. Should taste slightly sweeter than desired (freezing dulls sweetness).
8. **Pour** into Ninja Creami pint container to max fill line.
9. **Freeze** for 24 hours minimum. Must be frozen solid.
10. **Process** on ICE CREAM setting.
11. **Check texture.** Higher fat content should help — may only need 1 spin.
12. **Serve immediately** for soft-serve, or freeze 1–2 hours for scoopable.

## Success criteria

- Deep, rich chocolate flavor (not flat or chalky)
- Fudgy mouthfeel from real chocolate fat
- Tang reads as "dark chocolate depth" not "yogurt"
- Smooth texture, no graininess from cocoa

## Variables to track

- Chocolate richness (1-10) — compare to your existing chocolate protocols
- Tang perception (hidden / dark chocolate note / too yogurty)
- Texture/iciness (1-10)
- Sweetness level (1-10)
- Number of spins needed

## Potential adjustments

- If not chocolatey enough: increase baking chocolate to 25g
- If too tangy: reduce yogurt to 160g, increase coconut cream to 60g
- If too bitter: increase allulose to 40g, reduce cocoa to 12g
- If grainy: blend longer, or sift cocoa powder before adding
- If too sweet: reduce allulose to 30g

## Variations

- **Mexican chocolate:** Add 1g cinnamon + pinch cayenne
- **Mocha:** Increase espresso to 4g
- **Chocolate peanut butter:** Swirl 30g PB after processing
- **Chocolate sea salt:** Top with flaky salt after spinning
- **Double chocolate:** Fold in 30g sugar-free chips after processing

## Storage & reheat

- Keeps in freezer up to 2 weeks
- RE-SPIN if frozen solid; fat content should help it stay softer

# Protocol: Greek Yogurt Mango Lassi Ice Cream (Ninja Creami)

**Category:** desserts
**Tags:** #high-protein #batch-prep #low-mess
**Status:** BETA
**Version:** v0.1
**Servings / Yield:** 1 pint (4 servings, ~90g each)
**Prep time:** 5 min
**Freeze time:** 24 hours
**Tools:** Ninja Creami, blender

## Goal / Why this exists

Mango lassi-inspired frozen dessert. The yogurt tang is a feature here, not a bug — it's authentic to lassi flavor. Cardamom adds warmth and complexity.

## Hypothesis

Greek yogurt's tanginess pairs naturally with mango and cardamom. Frozen mango adds body, natural sweetness, and helps mask any base iciness.

## Nutrition (per serving, ~90g)

- **Calories:** ~85
- **Protein:** ~6g
- **Carbs:** ~14g
- **Fat:** ~1.5g

*Note: Using 0% Greek yogurt. Mango adds natural sugars.*

## Ingredients

- 180g Greek yogurt (0% fat)
- 60g lite coconut milk (or 45g full-fat coconut cream)
- 60g frozen mango chunks
- 25g allulose (reduced — mango adds sweetness)
- 3g vanilla extract (1/2 tsp)
- 1g ground cardamom (~1/2 tsp)
- Pinch kosher salt (Diamond Crystal)

## Method

1. **Blend** frozen mango with coconut milk until smooth.
2. **Add** yogurt, allulose, vanilla, cardamom, and salt.
3. **Blend** until fully incorporated and uniform color, ~45 seconds.
4. **Taste** for sweetness — adjust allulose if needed before freezing.
5. **Pour** into Ninja Creami pint container to max fill line.
6. **Freeze** for 24 hours minimum. Must be frozen solid.
7. **Process** on ICE CREAM setting.
8. **Check texture.** Mango fiber may help — might need only 1 spin.
9. **Serve immediately** for soft-serve, or freeze 1–2 hours for scoopable.

## Success criteria

- Distinct mango flavor, not muted
- Cardamom present but not overpowering
- Lassi-like tang that feels intentional
- Smoother than vanilla base (mango fiber helps)

## Variables to track

- Iciness level (1-10)
- Tang level (1-10) — should feel "lassi-like"
- Mango flavor intensity (1-10)
- Cardamom balance (too subtle / just right / overpowering)
- Number of spins needed

## Potential adjustments

- If mango too subtle: increase to 80g, reduce yogurt to 160g
- If cardamom overpowering: reduce to 0.5g
- If too icy: increase coconut milk to 75g or use coconut cream
- If too sweet: reduce allulose to 20g

## Variations

- **Mango chili:** Add pinch of cayenne for heat
- **Mango rose:** Add 1g rose water (use sparingly)
- **Mango ginger:** Add 3g fresh grated ginger

## Storage & reheat

- Keeps in freezer up to 2 weeks
- RE-SPIN if frozen solid
